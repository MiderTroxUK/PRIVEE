The presence of a second intern on an adjacent problem shaped the work more than expected.
Liam Jones was building the structural and geographic representation of a supply chain,
meaning how a network is described, generated and visualised, while this mission built the

rather than attempt to generate one, because that was being solved two seats away.

\subsection{Resources}
\label{sec:ressources}

The resources were modest, and one of them constrained technical choices. All implementation
was carried out by the author, on one ALTEN laptop, a Ryzen~7 PRO 8840HS with
16 cores and no discrete GPU, on which everything in this mission was computed, every model
training run included. Azure DevOps carried source control and work tracking. No budget was
managed, and none was requested: no paid data service, no cloud compute, no commercial solver.
That absence removed managed services and paid datasets from the solution space before any
technical comparison took place, which is why the delivered system depends only on open-source
Python libraries and a file-based database.

\subsection{Working Method: Agile and Test and Learn}
\label{sec:methode}

The internship agreement prescribes an evolutionary approach based on Agile and Test and
Learn. That was applied literally rather than nominally, and the weekly rhythm was the
mechanism.

\paragraph{The cadence.}
The week had a fixed shape, and Figure~\ref{fig:semaine} gives it together with the way it
nests inside the mission plan. Four contact points a week on a six-month mission is a heavy
cadence for R\&D, and it was the single most useful organisational feature of the internship:
it made it impossible to spend three weeks on an idea that would not survive contact with a
reviewer. It is also the mechanism that ties the week to the plan. A phase advances by one
week's increment, and the Friday demonstration is where that increment is accepted or the
phase is re-scoped, which is how the reallocation described in Section~\ref{sec:planning} was
decided.

\paragraph{Test and Learn, and what it rejected.}
The method means something only if things were abandoned. Four were. The first urgency
formulation went in April, for the reason given in Section~\ref{sec:problemes}. The May
pipeline of separate scores was superseded in June by the design that shipped, having first
established what the interfaces between components needed to be. A third-party logistics
dataset considered for the freight node was rejected on inspection, its pre-computed target
columns, misplaced coordinates and uniformly flat distributions all signatures of synthetic
data; the node was driven by calibrated events instead. And the human arm of the campaign was
reduced to isolated agents when eight consultants for nine days could not be secured, which
weakened every behavioural claim rather than the design (Section~\ref{sec:risques}).
