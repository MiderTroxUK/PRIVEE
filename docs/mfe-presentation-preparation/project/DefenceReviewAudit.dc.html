Section 3

Two of his points still open

He said
“Tu aurais pu prendre tous les stagiaires là et leur prendre une demi-heure chacun.”

This is the sharpest thing he said and the deck does not answer it. It lists “agents, not human consultants” as a limit and “run the campaign with humans” as the next step, but never says why you went to agents. Your answer in the room was availability. That is true and it is weak on its own.

The stronger answer is that the substitution is contested in the literature, so the agent campaign was never a stand-in for the human one. A 2025 review in Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science identifies six fallacies in substituting language models for human participants and concludes they complement rather than replace humans, supporting a simulation reading rather than a replacement one. A 2025 Nature Machine Intelligence paper ran 3,200 human participants against identity-prompted model outputs and found the models both misportray and flatten identity groups. And methodologists distinguish heuristic “validate then simulate” practice, useful for exploratory work, from statistical calibration, which is what confirmatory claims require.

Suggested line, one sentence on slide 37: “The agents made the loop fast enough to run at all; they were never a substitute for the human campaign, which the literature does not support and which remains the run that decides.”
He said
“Pourquoi là tu me dis juste, c'est pour ça que j'ai utilisé l'improbabilité… elle veut dire quoi, cette slide?”

He asked the same question about slide 19 that you asked me. His own answer was to fold it into the computation story: “pour l'urgence réelle on a pris l'improbabilité, on a pris PROMETHEE, on a pris…” In other words, the slide should not defend a formula. It should show what goes into the computed urgency, with the non-compensatory choice as one line of that story.

My recommendation: keep the slide, retitle it so it answers “what is inside Ur”, and let Node A against Node B be the evidence rather than the subject. That satisfies both of you and costs no time. Say the word and I will do it.
