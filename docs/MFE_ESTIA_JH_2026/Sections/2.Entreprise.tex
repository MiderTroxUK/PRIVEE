\subsection{The ALTEN Group}
\label{sec:alten}

ALTEN is a French engineering and technology consulting group founded in 1988, with its
head office in Boulogne-Billancourt. It sells engineering expertise rather than products:
its consultants are placed on the technical projects of client companies, across the
aerospace, defence, energy, transport, telecommunications, health and finance sectors. The
group operates in more than thirty countries, and France remains its largest market.

Two features of that model matter for understanding this mission. First, ALTEN's asset is
the technical capability of its people, so anything that raises the level of a whole
practice has leverage that a single client project does not. Second, a consulting group
sees the same problem recur across many clients, which puts it in an unusual position to
notice that a difficulty is structural rather than particular. The urgency assessment
problem treated in this report is of that kind.

\subsection{The Direction de l'Innovation}
\label{sec:din}

The \gls{din} is the entity responsible for ALTEN's own research and development, as
distinct from the work its consultants deliver to clients. It operates a network of ALTEN
Labs in France and abroad, and brings together permanent researchers, doctoral candidates,
consultants, interns and apprentices on multidisciplinary projects. Its output is not
billable hours but reusable assets: methods, prototypes and expertise that consultants and
clients can subsequently draw on.

This mission took place at the Toulouse Lab, within the Smart Green Supply Chain research
programme. The programme pursues three aims simultaneously. The lean aim targets waste,
meaning cost, delay and excess stock. The green aim targets the carbon footprint of each
transport leg, working to the \gls{glec} Framework v3.2~\autocite{smartfreight2023glec} and
ISO~14083:2023~\autocite{iso14083}. The predictive aim, where this work sits, targets the
anticipation of disruption.

The programme's central artefact is DISCO, a serious game and digital twin simulating a
complete supply chain for a satellite product across 22 component
suppliers~\autocite{baxas2025architecture}. Successive placements have built its components:
the Physical Internet routing module~\autocite{baulain2024internet}, the supplier risk
scorer~\autocite{kabouche2025scoring}, the per-route carbon
quantification~\autocite{cordova2024green} and the transport management
interface~\autocite{mondelice2025tms}. The work described here adds a layer none of them
covers, and it ran alongside a parallel placement on the network representation and
geographic modelling side of the same programme, which is discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:parties_prenantes}.

\subsection{Politique RSE d'ALTEN}
\label{sec:rse}

\begin{frenchBox}
\noindent\textbf{Ce paragraphe est r\'edig\'e en fran\c{c}ais, conform\'ement aux consignes
de r\'edaction du rapport de MFE.}

\medskip

ALTEN a adh\'er\'e au Pacte Mondial des Nations Unies en 2010 et structure depuis sa
politique de responsabilit\'e soci\'etale autour de trois piliers~: l'humain,
l'environnement et l'innovation durable. La d\'emarche n'est pas seulement d\'eclarative.
En 2025, le Groupe a obtenu un score EcoVadis de 85/100, niveau Platinum, qui le place dans
le premier centile mondial, et a confirm\'e sa note A\textminus{} au Carbon Disclosure
Project. Sa trajectoire de r\'eduction des \'emissions a \'et\'e valid\'ee par la Science
Based Targets initiative en 2023, avec un objectif de neutralit\'e carbone \`a l'horizon
2050.

Sur le pilier humain, le Groupe r\'eunit plus de cent nationalit\'es et a sign\'e les
\emph{Women's Empowerment Principles} des Nations Unies. L'index d'\'egalit\'e
professionnelle publi\'e au titre de 2025 s'\'el\`eve \`a 88/100 pour ALTEN Sud-Ouest,
l'entit\'e juridique dans laquelle s'est d\'eroul\'ee cette mission. Ce m\^eme document
montre que la repr\'esentation f\'eminine reste de 20~\% parmi les cadres dirigeants
d'ALTEN Sud-Ouest et de 14~\% au sein des instances dirigeantes, \'ecart que le Groupe
reconna\^it publiquement et qu'il entend traiter par le programme INSPIRE 2026-2030. Il
para\^it plus honn\^ete de citer cet \'ecart que de n'en retenir que l'index.

Sur le pilier environnemental, les actions internes sont concr\`etes et v\'erifiables~:
100~\% d'\'electricit\'e renouvelable pour les b\^atiments fran\c{c}ais dont la puissance
souscrite d\'epasse 36~kVA, 80~\% des surfaces couvertes par le tri s\'electif, et une
certification ISO~14001 en cours d'extension. La sensibilisation passe par des ateliers
Fresque du Climat et des formations aux \'eco-gestes.

C'est n\'eanmoins le troisi\`eme pilier qui \'eclaire le mieux cette mission. ALTEN
consacre 31~\% de sa R\&D \`a l'innovation environnementale, et le programme dans lequel ce
stage s'est inscrit en fait partie~: un programme nomm\'e \emph{Smart Green} Supply Chain,
o\`u les \'emissions de CO\textsubscript{2} constituent l'un des six blocs du mod\`ele
d'urgence d\'evelopp\'e ici, au m\^eme rang que le co\^ut ou le d\'elai, et non une
correction appliqu\'ee apr\`es coup. Autrement dit, la politique RSE du Groupe n'a pas
seulement encadr\'e ce travail~: elle en a d\'etermin\'e une variable de mod\'elisation.

\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Prise de recul.} Deux observations m\'eritent d'\^etre faites. D'abord, la
RSE d'ALTEN est nettement plus visible au niveau du Groupe qu'au niveau du quotidien d'un
laboratoire~: en six mois, je l'ai rencontr\'ee davantage sous la forme d'un objet de
recherche que sous celle d'une pratique de bureau. Ensuite, et c'est le point qui m'a le
plus marqu\'e, le bloc carbone du mod\`ele s'est r\'ev\'el\'e le moins sollicit\'e des six
lors de la campagne exp\'erimentale. Une entreprise peut donc afficher une politique
environnementale solide, financer un programme de recherche qui la porte, et produire
malgr\'e tout un mod\`ele o\`u la dimension environnementale reste la moins active, faute
de donn\'ees pour l'alimenter. L'\'ecart ne tient pas \`a l'intention mais \`a la
disponibilit\'e de la mesure. Il est signal\'e comme tel en
Section~\ref{sec:conclusion_limites}.
\end{frenchBox}

\paragraph{English translation of the above.}
ALTEN joined the United Nations Global Compact in 2010 and structures its corporate social
responsibility policy around three pillars: people, environment and sustainable innovation.
The approach is measured rather than merely stated. In 2025 the group scored 85/100 on
EcoVadis, reaching Platinum level and the top one per cent worldwide, and confirmed its
A\textminus{} rating with the Carbon Disclosure Project. Its emissions reduction pathway was
validated by the Science Based Targets initiative in 2023, targeting carbon neutrality by
2050.

On the people pillar, the group spans more than a hundred nationalities and is a signatory
of the UN Women's Empowerment Principles. The 2025 gender equality index stands at 88/100
for ALTEN Sud-Ouest, the legal entity in which this mission took place. The same publication
shows female representation still at 20\% among senior executives of ALTEN Sud-Ouest and
14\% on its governing bodies, a gap the group states publicly and intends to address through
its INSPIRE 2026-2030 programme. Citing that gap seems more honest than citing only the
index.

On the environmental pillar the internal measures are concrete and checkable: 100\%
renewable electricity for French buildings above 36~kVA of subscribed power, 80\% of floor
area covered by waste sorting, and ISO~14001 certification being extended. Awareness is
raised through Fresque du Climat workshops and eco-practice training.

It is the third pillar that bears most directly on this mission. ALTEN devotes 31\% of its
R\&D to environmental innovation, and the programme hosting this placement is part of that
share: a programme named \emph{Smart Green} Supply Chain, in which CO\textsubscript{2}
emissions form one of the six blocks of the urgency model developed here, ranked alongside
cost and delay rather than applied as an afterthought. The group's CSR policy did not merely
frame this work; it determined one of its modelling variables.

Two observations follow. ALTEN's CSR is considerably more visible at group level than in the
daily life of a laboratory: over six months I encountered it more as an object of research
than as an office practice. And the carbon block of the model turned out to be the least
exercised of the six during the experimental campaign. A company can therefore hold a solid
environmental policy, fund a research programme that carries it, and still produce a model
in which the environmental dimension is the least active one, for want of data to feed it.
The gap is not one of intent but of measurement availability, and it is recorded as such in
Section~\ref{sec:conclusion_limites}.
