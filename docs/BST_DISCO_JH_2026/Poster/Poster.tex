%--------------------------------------------------- CONTEXT
\begin{pbox}{1. Context \& Problem}
The \hl{DISCO digital twin} is one of the two bricks of ALTEN-DIN's
\emph{Smart Green Supply Chain} programme: a serious game scoring 22 suppliers
on cost, delay and CO$_2$.

\smallskip
Supply chains moving towards \hl{Physical Internet} operation face a
signal-quality gap — the urgency an operator \emph{declares} diverges from the
urgency that is \emph{physically computable} from the twin.

\smallskip
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[height=64mm]{img/urgency_gap.png}
\captionof{figure}{$U_d$ (step) vs.\ $U_r(t)$: false urgency $F$, hidden risk $H$.}
\end{center}

\smallskip
The two pathologies are \hl{not symmetric}: a coordinator who panics wastes
premium capacity; one who under-reports misses the window to prevent a line
stoppage.
\end{pbox}

\gap

%--------------------------------------------------- OBJECTIVES
\begin{pbox}{2. Objectives}
\begin{itemize}[label={}]
  \item[\textbf{O1.}] A multi-block, DAG-propagated real urgency $U_r(t)$ and a
        cognitive adequacy score $A(t)\in[0,100]$.
  \item[\textbf{O2.}] A network propagation and criticality layer
        (PROMETHEE II + systematic shock simulation) exposing structurally
        critical nodes.
  \item[\textbf{O3.}] Empirical validation of the prioritisation logic against
        recorded operational outcomes.
\end{itemize}

\smallskip
\begin{ochrebox}
Six \hl{pre-registered, falsifiable hypotheses} (H1–H6) fix every numeric
threshold \emph{before} any data exists: latency, early detection, criticality,
predictive validity, contagion, hysteresis.
\end{ochrebox}
\end{pbox}

\gap

%--------------------------------------------------- METHOD
\begin{pbox}{3. Method: a three-layer model}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[height=87mm]{img/three_layer_architecture.png}
\end{center}

\smallskip
\textbf{Real urgency} — noisy-OR over six KPI blocks (time, capacity,
performance, adverse-event risk, cost, environment), propagated on the
supply-chain DAG ($\beta$ upstream amplification, $\gamma$ downstream damping):
\begin{align*}
  U_{r,\text{loc}} &= 1-\textstyle\prod_{m}(1-u_m)^{\omega_m}\\
  U_{r,i} &= 1-(1-U_{r,\text{loc},i})\textstyle\prod_{j\in\mathrm{Pred}(i)}(1-\beta_{ji}U_{r,j})
\end{align*}

\smallskip
\textbf{Declared urgency} — pairwise elicitation over four expert criteria:
\hl{AHP} with a consistency-ratio gate, \hl{Fuzzy Best-Worst Method}.

\smallskip
\textbf{Adequacy} — Prospect-Theory asymmetric loss
($\lambda_{\text{under}}\!=\!2.25$, $\lambda_{\text{over}}\!=\!1$, $\alpha\!=\!0.88$):
\begin{gather*}
  F=[U_d-U_r]_+,\quad H=[U_r-U_d]_+,\quad
  p=\lambda_{\text{under}}H^{\alpha}+\lambda_{\text{over}}F^{\alpha}\\
  A = 100\,\bigl(e^{-p}-e^{-p_{\max}}\bigr)\big/\bigl(1-e^{-p_{\max}}\bigr)
\end{gather*}

\smallskip
\textbf{Criticality} — each node is knocked out in turn; the drop in the final
customer's urgency ranks the network (PROMETHEE II net flow $\phi$).
\end{pbox}

\gap

%--------------------------------------------------- IMPLEMENTATION
\begin{pbox}{4. Delivered system: \emph{SupplyScore}}
A persistent, \hl{multi-tenant digital twin} in Python on a weekly review cadence.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Single-facade architecture, gated writes, versioned SQLite (WAL,
        integrity-checked backups).
  \item Calibrated event engine — spikes, accidents, transport disruptions,
        shortages — with weekly Bayesian revision of failure probability.
  \item \hl{Exact additive explainability}: every point of $U_d$ and $U_r$ traced
        to its block or its upstream arc.
  \item Twelve-worksheet Excel export: analysis runs outside the tool on frozen data.
\end{itemize}
\end{pbox}

%--------------------------------------------------- VALIDATION
\begin{pbox}{5. Validation: the HÉLIOS serious game}
The \hl{2020–2022 semiconductor shortage} replayed as an 8-node, 18-turn serious
game over nine working days. $U_r$ is fed by public series (INSEE, WSTS) frozen
under SHA-256; $U_d$ comes from the players' weekly AHP questionnaire. Protocol
frozen before turn 1, with placebo sheets and a weak-signal turn as controls.

\smallskip
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[height=78mm]{img/campaign_network.png}
\captionof{figure}{The eight HÉLIOS nodes and the public series feeding $U_r$.}
\end{center}

\smallskip
A \textbf{synthetic dry run} (5 iterations) calibrated the null baseline
(precision 0.16 / recall 0.27); an exploratory \textbf{pilot with eight isolated
LLM agents} then ran the full production pipeline.

\smallskip
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[height=68mm]{img/helios_llm_pilot_adequacy.png}
\captionof{figure}{Network-average adequacy $A$ across the 18 turns of the LLM
pilot, annotated with the calibrated crisis events (five acts: baseline, tipping
point, squeeze, rupture, recovery).}
\end{center}

\smallskip
{\fontsize{18}{22}\selectfont
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.42\linewidth}p{0.21\linewidth}p{0.30\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Hypothesis} & \textbf{Verdict} & \textbf{Measured} \\
\midrule
H1 Cognitive latency      & Not confirmed & 3 of 8 nodes (thr.\ 5) \\
H2 Early detection        & Not confirmed & $H_{\max}\!=\!0.017$ (thr.\ 0.3) \\
H3 Structural criticality & Not confirmed & top-1 on 70\% (thr.\ 80\%) \\
H4 Predictive validity    & Not confirmed & precision / recall 0.00 \\
\textbf{H5 Urgency contagion} & \textbf{Confirmed} & $+0.079$ vs.\ $+0.005$ \\
H6 Perception hysteresis  & Not applicable & no measurable decline \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}

\smallskip
\begin{keybox}
\textbf{Key result.} Urgency contagion is reproduced at network scale: nodes
\emph{not} physically affected still raise their declared urgency on press-event
turns. The LLM readers track their briefing too faithfully to leave the gap $H$
is built to catch — which is why \textbf{the human campaign is the only fair test
of H4}.
\end{keybox}
\end{pbox}

\gap

%--------------------------------------------------- BRICK 2
\begin{pbox}{6. Second brick: warehouse volumetry at scale}
The Physical Internet needs to know \emph{where} storage capacity sits, of
\emph{what type} and \emph{what volume} — at a scale no manual survey reaches.
This brick characterises any warehouse from its \hl{GPS coordinates alone}.

\smallskip
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.30\linewidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth,clip,trim=0 70 70 0]{img/warehouse_satellite.png}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.67\linewidth}
\vspace{-3mm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item ESRI satellite tiles, zoom 18 ($\approx$0.6\,m/px), 3$\times$3 grid.
  \item Ground resolution \hl{calibrated} on the OSM footprint and the 2.40\,m
        standard truck width.
  \item \hl{SAM\,3} text-prompted segmentation: \emph{warehouse}, \emph{loading
        dock}, \emph{truck}, \emph{office}.
  \item Shape class I / L / U from solidity and compactness.
  \item Storage volume $=$ footprint $\times$ height (OSM \texttt{height}, or
        levels $\times\,3.5$\,m).
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

\smallskip
One JSON record per site — surface, docks, trucks, shape, volume — i.e.
\hl{indicators that aggregate into a national storage-capacity map} and feed the
twin with \emph{observed} capacity instead of declared capacity.
\end{pbox}

\gap

%--------------------------------------------------- CONCLUSION
\begin{pbox}{7. Conclusions \& next steps}
A multi-block real urgency, an asymmetric cognitive adequacy score and an operable
digital twin were delivered; the measurement apparatus around them is frozen and
pre-registered.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Run the human HÉLIOS campaign — the only run able to decide H1–H6.
  \item Recalibrate $(\lambda_{\text{under}},\lambda_{\text{over}},\alpha)$ on real
        coordinators, not on literature defaults.
  \item Connect an ERP feed, so score freshness stops depending on manual entry.
  \item Fuse the two bricks: observed volumetry as a physical input to the
        capacity block of $U_r$.
\end{itemize}
\end{pbox}
