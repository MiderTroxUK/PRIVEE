This R\&D operation delivered \emph{SupplyScore}: a multi-block, noisy-OR real urgency model propagated across the supply chain DAG, an asymmetric cognitive adequacy score confronting declared and computed urgency, and a persistent, multi-tenant digital twin operated as a weekly-cadence serious game, with a pre-registered validation campaign (Section~\ref{sec:serious_game}) sized to measure the predictive validity of the hidden-risk signal. The scope of the delivered framework was bounded by explicit, documented design decisions rather than left implicit. This section states those limitations directly, draws the next steps, and closes with a personal assessment of the internship.

\textbf{Limitation} & \textbf{Implication} \\

\subsection{Next Steps}
%% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The immediate next step of the programme is to run the human campaign itself (Section~\ref{sec:serious_game}). Role assignment across the eight consultants is not yet finalised and must be recorded in the campaign journal before the first turn, deliberately keeping the two most information-heavy roles, NovaFab and CompoDis, away from the most supply-chain-experienced consultants, so that a role effect is not confused with an expertise effect. A half-day kickoff, presentation, role cards, a signed contract, and a training turn, formally freezes the protocol; the eighteen campaign turns then run over nine working days with a daily journal and the two placebo sheets at turns 9 and 15, and the campaign closes with the pre-registered analysis and a reveal debrief that asks each consultant at which turn they recognised the real crisis.

That analysis will fill the two reserved needs left open by Section~\ref{sec:serious_game}: the measured value of each of H1 to H6 against its pre-registered threshold, a verdict of supported, refuted, or inconclusive for each, and a sensitivity check (exclusion of absent consultants, a $\pm 50\%$ perturbation of the constants fixed by design) confirming whether those verdicts hold. Where the campaign supports a hypothesis but the underlying governance parameters, $\lambda_{\text{under}}$, $\alpha$, the block weights $\omega_m$, and the Bayesian prior weight $n_0$, fit the measured data poorly, recalibrating them from campaign data rather than from the literature-derived defaults of Section~\ref{sec:math_framework} is the direct follow-on. Three contingencies are already arbitrated rather than left to be improvised under time pressure: the campaign truncates to turn 16 if it overruns by more than two turns rather than rushing the remaining responses, it drops to one turn per day if the questionnaire exceeds ten minutes at the training turn, and any consultant absence or unresolved consistency failure is carried forward unchanged and excluded from reactivity analyses.

Beyond the campaign itself, three further directions were identified but deliberately left out of the delivered scope: replacing single-operator AHP elicitation with a multi-expert protocol that aggregates several judgments per node, coupling SupplyScore with the DISCO platform it currently runs alongside rather than inside (Section~\ref{sec:Context}), and using the adequacy score as an input to scheduling decisions rather than only as a diagnostic. None of the three is a design commitment yet: each depends on what the human campaign shows about whether the underlying signal is worth building on.

\subsubsection{Compétences techniques mobilisées}

Cette opération de R\&D a mobilisé des compétences dans quatre domaines distincts, dont l'articulation constitue l'essentiel de la difficulté technique du sujet~:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Aide à la décision multi-critères} : élicitation par comparaisons par paires (AHP, Fuzzy-BWM), validation de cohérence, agrégation par surclassement (PROMETHEE~II).
    \item \textbf{Modélisation probabiliste} : agrégation noisy-OR, simulation de Monte Carlo, révision bayésienne conjuguée (Beta-Bernoulli) en régime de données rares.
    \item \textbf{Algorithmique de graphes} : propagation sur graphe orienté acyclique multi-rangs, tri topologique, simulation de choc et calcul de criticité réseau.
    \item \textbf{Génie logiciel} : architecture d'un système multi-tenant en Python, persistance SQLite durcie (WAL, sauvegarde, migrations), interface web Dash, démarche qualité en jalons avec couverture de tests bloquante et tests de bout en bout automatisés.
\end{itemize}
