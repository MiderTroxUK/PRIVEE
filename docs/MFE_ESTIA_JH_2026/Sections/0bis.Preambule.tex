\paragraph{Status of the results.}
This report is submitted on 24 August 2026, with two months of a six-month internship still to
run. One strand is deliberately left open: the tool is being run against additional
supply chains to widen the evidence base behind every rate quoted here, and
Appendix~\ref{app:planning} states what the remaining time carries. The construction, the
measurements and the diagnoses are stable and reproducible from the artefacts listed in
Section~\ref{sec:objectifs}. The rates attached to them are those of the campaigns completed
to date and may move as that base widens.

\paragraph{Confidentiality.}
The project is designated confidential by ALTEN in the annex to the internship agreement.
This report carries a confidentiality marking on every page. It contains internal scoring
parameters, the architecture of an unreleased software product and results not published
elsewhere.
